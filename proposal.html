What we build

Three technologies. One goal: give people back control.

Every design file, firmware build and clinical protocol we create is published openly, so a clinic in Nairobi can build what a lab in Boston builds.

Prosthetics

Low-cost myoelectric hands, 3D-printed sockets and passively adaptive feet. Built from parts you can source locally, repaired with a hex key.

Exoskeletons

Lightweight hip and knee assistance for spinal cord injury, stroke rehab and industrial fatigue — with torque tuned to the wearer, not the average.

Human-machine interfaces

Surface EMG, IMU fusion and open SDKs that let a wearer retrain their own device. Signal processing that runs on a $6 microcontroller.
